The Problem of World Hunger

World hunger is a complex issue that affects millions of people worldwide. It is caused by a variety of factors, including poverty, inequality, and conflict. Addressing world hunger requires a multifaceted approach that takes into account the root causes of the problem. Death becomes a reality for many, with millions suffering from malnutrition and food insecurity. For some, the only way to eat is from food banks. The Cold Hard Facts of World Hunger

  • According to the United Nations, approximately 9% of the world's population is undernourished.
  • Children are the most affected, with 1 in 5 children stunted due to malnutrition.
  • Conflict and violence are significant drivers of hunger, displacing millions and disrupting food systems.
  • Climate change poses a growing threat to food security, affecting crop yields and access to resources.
