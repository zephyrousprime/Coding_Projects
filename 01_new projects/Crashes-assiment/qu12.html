Saturday is the most dangerous day to drive

Saturday records the highest number of fatal crashes at 9,987 18.3% of all crashes, followed by Friday at 8,973 16.4%. Together, the weekend days account for over a third of all fatal crashes. Monday is the safest day with just 6,299 (11.5%). It can also be seen that there is a pattern of increased crashs as the week goes on but this patteren stops on Sunday where it has less chrashes than Saturday and Friday but not Thursday. The decrease in fatal crashes on Sunday might be attributed to reduced traffic and improved driving conditions because more people are likely to be at home or just do one small trip.

The Christmas period is more dangerous than Easter

The Christmas holiday period sees significantly more fatal crashes than Easter, likely due to the longer holiday window, increased road travel, and higher rates of impaired driving during the festive season.
